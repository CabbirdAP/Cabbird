## Data and privacy

Cabbird enables basic usage statistics by default, solely for anonymous statistics on daily active users, daily users of each plugin, and usage among Chinese- and English-language users. No log files are uploaded.

## When something breaks

If the game crashes, export the log from **Diagnostics** before reporting it; that is what makes the failing step identifiable.
